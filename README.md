## The entry

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes one funny one-star
review, in the voice of a house cat about its human, to `/output/entry.txt`:

> One star. She moved my favourite sun spot by two inches and expects me to just adapt. I have adapted. I am on her pillow.

## Run

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
